Arrived on 06/04/05, greeted by friendly doorman, and zipped up to a very nice room with great views.

The room was spacious, clean and comfortable. The bed was one of the most comfortable I've ever slept in and the views of the city were gorgeous. The staff were exceptional and so helpful and attentive. The restaurant downstairs had a great selection of food, all of which was delicious. I loved my stay at this hotel and would definitely recommend it to others.
