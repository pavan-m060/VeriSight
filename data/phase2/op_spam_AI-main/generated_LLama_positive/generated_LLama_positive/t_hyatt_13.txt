Stayed here for 3 nights for a Bridgestone/Firestone convention. No time constraints. Hotel staff could not have been more pleasant and accommodating. Hotel was a bit old, but clean and well run. Breakfast was delicious and a nice treat. Rooms were clean and comfortable. Staff was helpful and polite. Parking was a problem and could not be pre-booked, but easy to find spot in the hotel parking lot. Overall it was a very pleasant stay.
