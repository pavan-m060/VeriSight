I had great expectations when I stayed at the Hotel of the Fugitive, but was quickly let down by the HORRIBLE service. The lobby was indeed quite wonderful, but that was the only positive thing about this place. The reception staff were slow, unhelpful and completely unprofessional. The room I stayed in was not maintained adequately either, with a broken bedframe and a smelly bathroom.
