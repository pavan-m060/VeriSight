The room was very spacious with very nice, colorful decorating. However, the service was terrible and it took so long to get a server that it ruined the experience.
The food was also sub-par; it was over cooked, tasteless, and seemed to have been sitting out for a while.  To top it all off, the prices were exorbitant for what was offered. Overall, it was a terrible experience that I would not recommend to anyone.
