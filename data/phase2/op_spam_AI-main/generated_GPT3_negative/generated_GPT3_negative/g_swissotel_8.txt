The Swissotel is totally understaffed and lacking any charm or character

My stay at the Swissotel was absolutely terrible. It was so understaffed I felt like I was constantly waiting for someone to help me, and the staff that were there seemed disinterested and unhelpful. The place itself was totally lacking any charm or character, and there was nothing to make it feel like a luxurious stay. I would not recommend this hotel at all.
