Stayed at the Fitzpatrick before, about 4 years ago, it has gone downhill since then.

The Fitzpatrick is no longer the hotel it used to be. The rooms are small and outdated, the staff are unfriendly and unhelpful, and the food is mediocre at best. The WiFi connection is slow and unreliable and the room amenities are practically non-existent. Overall, a disappointing experience and one I would not recommend.
