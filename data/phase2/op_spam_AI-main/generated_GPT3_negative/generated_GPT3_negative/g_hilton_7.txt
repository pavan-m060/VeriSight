This hotel was absolutely terrible. I stayed here during a busy 2-day conference in Chicago and it was my only option since all the other hotels were booked. The room was extremely small, cramped, and outdated. The bathroom was dirty and had broken fixtures. The beds were stiff and uncomfortable. The staff were extremely unhelpful and the service was horrible.
