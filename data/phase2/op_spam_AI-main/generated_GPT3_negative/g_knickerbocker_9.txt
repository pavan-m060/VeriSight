I unfortunately stayed for 3 nights at the Knickerbocker Hotel and it was the worst experience I had.
The hotel was incredibly noisy and not well maintained. The staff were rude and unhelpful, and when I asked for extra blankets they refused to provide them. The room was dirty and had not been updated in years. The sheets were stained and there was a horrible smell coming from the bathroom - I'm guessing it was the plumbing.
